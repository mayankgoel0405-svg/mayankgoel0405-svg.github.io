Mewing guide

Mewing is resting your tongue on the roof of your mouth all day, not only during exercises.

  1. Lay your whole tongue flat on the roof of your mouth, including the back part.
  2. Keep the tip just behind your front teeth, not touching them.
  3. Close your lips and breathe through your nose.
  4. Keep your teeth lightly together or slightly apart, and your head level.

    These exercises build muscle tone and improve head and neck posture, which can change how your jaw and chin look. They can't change your bone structure, and there is little evidence that mewing reshapes an adult face. Its real value is a better resting posture and nose breathing. Stop if you feel pain or your jaw clicks or locks, and see a dentist or doctor if it continues.
